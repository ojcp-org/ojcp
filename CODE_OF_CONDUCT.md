# Code of Conduct

OJCP adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

The full text is available at: <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

## Summary

Participants in the OJCP project — across GitHub issues, pull requests, mailing lists, and any official channels — are expected to:

- Engage respectfully and constructively
- Assume good faith
- Welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels
- Focus on what is best for the standard and the broader ecosystem

OJCP brings together representatives of competing companies. Treat that as the asset it is — different perspectives produce a better standard.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to:

- **Primary:** `ojcp-conduct@recruitics.com`
- **Bootstrap-period escalation (if the report involves the Recruitics seat-holder):** `conduct-escalation@ojcp.dev` — this alias will be monitored by an external reviewer who is not a Recruitics employee and not currently a steering committee nominee.

> **Status (2026-04):** The external escalation reviewer is being identified. Until appointed and announced via a decision record in `docs/decisions/`, escalations sent to `conduct-escalation@ojcp.dev` will be triaged by a Recruitics employee outside of OJCP work, and the reporter will be informed of the interim handling. The external reviewer is a hard prerequisite before the first non-Recruitics steering committee seat is filled.

Reports are handled confidentially. All reports — including those handled via the escalation path — are summarized (with personal details redacted) in an annual transparency report. The first transparency report is due 12 months after the second steering committee seat is filled, and is the responsibility of the seated committee (during bootstrap, this is the responsibility of the Recruitics seat-holder, with external-reviewer co-sign once one is appointed).

## Enforcement

The steering committee enforces this code of conduct using the [Contributor Covenant Enforcement Ladder](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines). Actions range from a private warning to a permanent ban, depending on severity and pattern.

**Bootstrap-period exception.** While the steering committee has fewer than 4 seated members, conduct reports are reviewed by:
- The seated steering committee members not involved in the incident, AND
- The external escalation reviewer (above)

Both must independently agree on enforcement actions during the bootstrap period. This dual-review requirement removes the structural conflict of having Recruitics adjudicate conduct complaints involving Recruitics. Once the committee is fully seated (7 of 7), enforcement reverts to standard committee-only review with a recused-member requirement.
